How I sell

Four habits, learned the long way.

  1. Listen first

    Discovery isn't a checkbox. I dig until I understand the real problem — the one behind the one you told me about.

  2. Solve, don't pitch

    If the fit isn't there, I'll say so. The deals I close stick because they were the right call for both sides.

  3. Close with clarity

    No pressure theatrics. Clear terms, clean next steps, and a decision everyone feels good about on Monday.

  4. Grow the account

    The signature is the starting line. Renewals, referrals, and expansion come from showing up after the sale.
